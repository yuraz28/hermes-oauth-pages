Hermes Agent — Google Workspace access

Personal automation client for a single Google account

This is a personal, single-user application. It is a locally installed agent (Hermes Agent, running with the open-source Google Workspace MCP server) that the account owner uses on his own machine to work with his own Google Workspace data: Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Gmail, Calendar, Tasks, Contacts, Forms and Apps Script.

There are no other users. Nothing is published, resold or shared. All data stays between the owner's computer and Google's APIs; a call is made only when the owner asks the agent to do something.

What the app does

  • reads and searches files on the owner's Google Drive;
  • creates and edits Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms;
  • reads, drafts and sends email from the owner's own Gmail account when asked;
  • reads and creates calendar events and tasks;
  • reads contacts for lookup by name.

Privacy

The app stores OAuth tokens locally in a file readable only by the owner's macOS account (~/.google_workspace_mcp/credentials/). No data is sent to any third-party server, analytics service or advertiser.
